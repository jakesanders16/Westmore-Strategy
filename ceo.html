Jake Sanders is the CEO of Westmere Strategy Group, where he focuses on connecting athletes with brands and helping brands earn meaningful exposure through sports.

His experience spans club-level sponsorship development, local team activations, and relationship-building across the sports and business communities. He brings an energetic, hands-on approach to finding shared value and turning introductions into partnerships.

At the center of Westmere is a simple idea: the right connection can create opportunity for everyone involved.
